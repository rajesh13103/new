Happy Teachers’ Day 🌸

To a teacher who inspires, guides, and makes learning meaningful…

A great teacher doesn’t just teach lessons…

They leave lessons in our lives.

Thank you for every lesson, every correction, every encouragement, and every little moment that helped me become better.

Wait… before I continue.

There is actually something I’ve been wanting to say.

I’m Sorry. ❤

Sorry for the little moments when I may have disappointed you…

Sorry for the times when I could have done a little better…

Sorry for the moments when I didn’t understand what you were trying to tell me…

And sorry for every little thing I may have done without realizing how it felt to you.

Maybe these were only little things…

But I still wanted to say it.

I’m truly sorry. ❤

But that’s not the whole reason I said sorry…

There’s something much more important I want you to know.

Because today…

I don’t want to wish you only as my teacher.

I want to wish you as my AKKA ❤

You are not just someone who teaches me.

You are someone who guides me.

Someone who corrects me.

Someone who supports me.

Someone I can look up to.

And that makes you much more special to me.

Happy Teachers’ Day, Akka ❤

Thank you for being my teacher.

Thank you for being my guide.

And most importantly…

Thank you for being my Akka. 🌸

♥

Some people teach us lessons.

Some people become part of our lives.

You’re both.

With lots of respect, gratitude & love ❤
